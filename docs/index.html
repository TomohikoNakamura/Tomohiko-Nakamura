Profile

Tomohiko Nakamura received B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. degrees from the University of Tokyo, Japan, in 2011, 2013, and 2016. He joined SECOM Intelligent Systems Laboratory as a researcher in 2016 and moved to the University of Tokyo as Project Research Associate in September 2019. He is a senior researcher in Intelligent Media Processing Research Team, Artificial Intelligence Research Center, National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST).
