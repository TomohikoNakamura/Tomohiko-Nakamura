Hyperbolic Embeddings for Musical Timbre and Audio Effects

We investigate hyperbolic representations for structure-aware music and audio processing. Applications include timbre embeddings for musical-instrument sound synthesis with variational autoencoders and order-aware classification of audio effect chains.

Related Publications

  • Aogu Wada, Tomohiko Nakamura, and Hiroshi Saruwatari, “Hyperbolic embeddings for order-aware classification of audio effect chains,” in Proceedings of International Conference on Digital Audio Effects, Sept. 2025, pp. 396–402.
  • Futa Nakashima, Tomohiko Nakamura, Norihiro Takamune, Satoru Fukayama, and Hiroshi Saruwatari, “Hyperbolic timbre embedding for musical instrument sound synthesis based on variational autoencoders,” in Proceedings of Asia Pacific Signal and Information Processing Association Annual Summit and Conference, Nov. 2022, pp. 736–743.
