Variable-Time-Resolution Neural Audio Codec

We investigate neural audio codecs whose token temporal resolution can be adjusted within a single model. Sampling-frequency-independent convolutional layers adapt analysis and synthesis operations across token rates. The codec is one concrete realization of the broader direction of variable-time-resolution audio processing.

Related Publications

  • Tomohiko Nakamura, Wataru Nakata, Kanami Imamura, and Yuki Saito, “Neural audio codec with adjustable token temporal resolution using sampling-frequency-independent convolutional layers,” in Proceedings of International Workshop on Acoustic Signal Enhancement, Sept. 2026.
