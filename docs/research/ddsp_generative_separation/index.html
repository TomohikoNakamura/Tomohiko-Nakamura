Neural Analysis-by-Synthesis Based on DDSP Mixture Models

We develop analysis-by-synthesis methods for mixtures of harmonic sounds using differentiable digital signal processing (DDSP) mixture models. Neural inference estimates the synthesis parameters of constituent sounds from a mixture. Recent work incorporates diffusion-based estimation, and the same parameterized representation supports score-informed music-audio editing.
