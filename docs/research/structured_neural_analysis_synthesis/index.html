Filterbank-Inspired Neural Analysis–Synthesis

We investigate filterbank-inspired neural analysis–synthesis structures, including their application to audio source separation. Multiresolution Deep Layered Analysis (MRDLA) draws on wavelet-inspired multiresolution analysis. A distinct approach uses orthogonal convolutional layers to construct invertible analysis–masking–synthesis networks. The invertibility guarantee applies to the orthogonal-convolution approach, not to MRDLA.
