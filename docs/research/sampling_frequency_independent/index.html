Sampling-Frequency-Independent Audio Source Separation

Deep-learning-based audio source separation usually assumes a fixed sampling frequency. We develop sampling-frequency-independent convolutional layers and neural analog filters, including stride conversion, so that source-separation networks can operate under sampling frequencies not seen during training. We also investigate their robustness to sampling-frequency mismatch.
