Speech Representation Learning and Model Analysis

We investigate learned speech representations and efficient speech modeling, including self-supervised and generative spoken language models. Topics include extracting discrete speech units using independent component analysis (ICA), comparing phonetic and semantic information in representations, analyzing sensitivity to pretraining languages with neural audio codec tokens, and studying how generative spoken language models encode noisy speech. As a separate research direction, SelfRemaster addresses self-supervised restoration of degraded recordings, especially historical audio, using analysis-by-synthesis with source, filter, and recording-channel modeling without paired clean references.

The speech-representation studies include joint work with Prof. Shinji Watanabe (CMU)
