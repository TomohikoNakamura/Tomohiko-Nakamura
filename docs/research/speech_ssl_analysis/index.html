Self-Supervised Models for Efficient Speech Modeling and Analysis

We study self-supervised speech models and learned representations for compact speech modeling and analysis. This research includes extracting discrete speech units through independent component analysis (ICA), comparing the phonetic and semantic information in speech representations, and examining sensitivity to pretraining languages using neural audio codec tokens (Takizawa et al., Interspeech 2026).

Joint work with Prof. Shinji Watanabe (CMU)
