Neural and Physics-Informed Spatial Audio Processing

We investigate spatial audio processing from limited acoustic measurements, including neural interpolation of head-related transfer functions (HRTFs) across source directions and sparse sound-field estimation from magnitude-only or complex observations. For sound-field estimation, physics-informed neural networks and flow matching provide distinct ways to incorporate measurement and physical constraints.

Joint work with Prof. Shoichi Koyama (NII) and collaborators
