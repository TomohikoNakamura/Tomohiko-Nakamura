Real-Time Blind Speaker Extraction

We develop low-latency multichannel blind speech extraction for real acoustic environments, focusing on directional speech embedded in diffuse noise. The methods combine spatially regularized independent low-rank matrix analysis (ILRMA) with rank-constrained spatial covariance matrix estimation (RCSCME), and investigate fast demixing updates, blockwise and online processing, and asymmetric analysis windows for real-time operation.
