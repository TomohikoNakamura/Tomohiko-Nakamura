Vocal Ensemble Separation and Synthesis for Contemporary A Cappella

We study separation and synthesis of contemporary a cappella vocal ensembles, where several singers interact and perform simultaneously. The jaCappella corpus provides separately recorded Japanese vocal parts and annotations for this research. We also investigate lead-vocal separation from vocal-ensemble mixtures using phoneme-alignment information.
